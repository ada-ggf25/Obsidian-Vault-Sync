Generally: **no** — not in the direct “same family” sense.

### How they relate to Marxism

**Marxism** is built around:

- class conflict (capital vs labor),
    
- critique/abolition of capitalism,
    
- collective ownership (in many Marxist traditions),
    
- skepticism about liberal “rights” as _formal_ freedoms that can hide economic domination.
    

That puts it in pretty strong tension with both **classical liberalism** and **libertarianism**, which typically:

- defend **private property** and **markets**,
    
- treat **individual rights** and **limited state coercion** as central.
    

### Liberalism vs Marxism

- **Classical liberalism**: usually _anti-Marxist_ (pro-capitalism, property rights).
    
- **Modern/social liberalism**: still _not Marxist_, but it can **overlap on some goals** (worker protections, welfare state, reducing inequality). The justification is different: liberals usually want to **reform** capitalism, not replace it.
    

### Libertarianism vs Marxism

- **Right-libertarianism** (the common one): basically _opposite_ of Marxism on economics (strong property rights, minimal redistribution).
    
- **Left-libertarianism / libertarian socialism**: this is the _one place_ you get a real connection.
    
    - It shares Marxism’s anti-capitalist vibe (or at least anti-private-ownership-of-productive-capital),
        
    - but it rejects authoritarian state control and emphasizes decentralization and individual freedom.
        
    - Related currents: **anarchism**, **anarcho-syndicalism**, **council communism**.
        

So if by “libertarianism” you mean the common US usage, it’s **not Marxist**. But if you mean the broader historical/European sense where “libertarian” can mean **anti-authoritarian left**, then **yes, there’s overlap**.

### Quick cheat sheet

- **Classical liberalism** ↔ Marxism: **mostly conflict**
    
- **Modern liberalism** ↔ Marxism: **some shared concerns**, different foundations
    
- **Right-libertarianism** ↔ Marxism: **mostly conflict**
    
- **Left-libertarianism / libertarian socialism** ↔ Marxism: **partial overlap**
