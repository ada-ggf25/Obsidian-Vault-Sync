#philosophy #politics 

Here’s a **broad, fairly comprehensive map of political ideologies**, grouped by “families.” (Names overlap a lot in real life, and parties often mix several.)

## Liberal and Enlightenment traditions

- **Liberalism** (classical / modern / social) — rights, rule of law, constitutionalism; markets + varying welfare/regulation.
    
- **Neoliberalism** — market-oriented policy agenda within liberal-democratic institutions.
    
- **Libertarianism** (right / left) — maximize individual liberty; minimal state (right) or anti-capitalist decentralism (left).
    
- **Egalitarian / Rawlsian liberalism** — liberal rights + strong fairness/equal-opportunity commitments.
    
- **Republicanism (civic)** — freedom as non-domination; civic virtue, mixed government, anti-corruption.
    
- **Liberal democracy** — competitive elections + civil liberties + checks and balances.
    
- **Constitutionalism** — emphasis on limiting power through constitutions/courts.
    

## Conservative and traditionalist families

- **Conservatism** — preserve institutions/traditions; incremental change.
    
- **Traditionalism** — stronger emphasis on inherited social order, religion, hierarchy.
    
- **Social conservatism** — traditional norms on family/culture.
    
- **Fiscal conservatism** — low taxes/spending, balanced budgets.
    
- **National conservatism** — nation-first, cultural cohesion, skepticism of globalism.
    
- **One-nation / paternalistic conservatism** — social stability + some welfare.
    
- **Christian democracy** — conservative social roots + social-market/welfare orientation (varies).
    

## Socialist, communist, and labor traditions

- **Socialism** (broad) — social/worker ownership or strong economic democracy; anti-capitalist to mixed.
    
- **Social democracy** — regulated capitalism + strong welfare state + unions.
    
- **Democratic socialism** — socialism pursued through democratic institutions.
    
- **Marxism** — class analysis, critique of capitalism; many branches.
    
- **Marxism–Leninism** — vanguard party + centralized state socialism.
    
- **Maoism** — revolutionary strategy emphasizing peasantry/mass line; party-led.
    
- **Trotskyism** — permanent revolution; critique of bureaucratic socialism.
    
- **Council communism** — worker councils; anti-party centralism.
    
- **Syndicalism / anarcho-syndicalism** — unions as basis of governance/economy.
    

## Anarchist and radical-liberty traditions

- **Anarchism** (broad) — opposition to coercive hierarchy; bottom-up organization.
    
- **Anarcho-communism** — common ownership + stateless organization.
    
- **Mutualism** — cooperative markets, anti-capitalist property norms.
    
- **Individualist anarchism** — strong individual autonomy; anti-state.
    
- **Anarcho-capitalism** — stateless order with private property and markets (contested within anarchist history).
    

## Nationalist and state-sovereignty ideologies

- **Civic nationalism** — nation as shared citizenship/values.
    
- **Ethnic nationalism** — nation defined by ancestry/ethnicity (more exclusionary).
    
- **Pan-nationalism** — unite a broader “people” across borders (pan-Arabism, pan-Slavism, etc.).
    
- **Separatism / secessionism** — independence for a region/people.
    
- **Irredentism** — reclaim “lost” territories.
    
- **Anti-colonial nationalism** — sovereignty and decolonization.
    
- **Zionism** (many strands) — Jewish national movement; ranges from liberal to religious-nationalist variants.
    

## Populism and “anti-establishment” styles (often cross-ideological)

- **Populism** — “the people” vs “the elite” framing; can be left, right, or centrist.
    
- **Left populism** — anti-oligarchy, redistribution.
    
- **Right populism** — nativism/sovereigntism often combined with cultural conservatism.
    

## Authoritarian, hierarchical, and regime-centered ideologies

- **Authoritarianism** — political power concentrated; limited pluralism.
    
- **Totalitarianism** — state seeks control over most of life (stronger than authoritarianism).
    
- **Monarchism** (absolute / constitutional) — monarchy as core institution.
    
- **Absolutism** — unchecked sovereign authority.
    
- **Oligarchy / plutocracy** — rule by few / by wealth (more descriptive, sometimes ideological defenses exist).
    
- **Militarism** — military values/priority in governance.
    
- **Corporatism** — society organized by state-recognized sector groups (labor, business, etc.).
    
- **Fascism** (historical family) — ultranationalist, authoritarian, anti-liberal, mass-mobilizing politics.
    
- **Nazism** — fascism with explicit racial ideology and genocidal program (historical).
    

## Religious political ideologies

- **Theocracy** — rule justified by religious authority/law.
    
- **Islamism** (many forms) — political order grounded in Islamic principles (ranges widely).
    
- **Hindu nationalism** — politics centered on Hindu civilizational identity (varies).
    
- **Christian theocratic movements** — governance explicitly grounded in Christian doctrine (varies).
    
- **Religious fundamentalism** (across faiths) — literalist/tradition-maximizing politics.
    

## Green, ecological, and sustainability ideologies

- **Green politics** — ecology, social justice, grassroots democracy.
    
- **Eco-socialism** — ecological goals + socialist economics.
    
- **Eco-liberalism** — environmental protection within liberal market frameworks.
    
- **Deep ecology** — nature-centered ethics; can inspire varied politics.
    

## Feminist and equality-centered ideologies

- **Liberal feminism** — equal rights/opportunity via law and institutions.
    
- **Socialist feminism** — gender + class analysis; economic structures matter.
    
- **Radical feminism** — patriarchy as primary structure; deep social transformation.
    
- **Intersectional feminism** — overlapping systems of oppression (race/class/gender, etc.).
    

## Identity, culture, and community-centered ideologies

- **Multiculturalism** — recognition and accommodation of diverse groups.
    
- **Communitarianism** — community obligations/values alongside individual rights.
    
- **Civic communitarian republicanism** — citizenship, participation, shared civic norms.
    

## Global order ideologies

- **Internationalism** — cooperation across borders; rules-based order.
    
- **Cosmopolitanism** — moral/political concern extends globally, beyond nations.
    
- **Globalism** (broad label) — deep integration of economies/institutions (used both positively and negatively).
    

## Governance and “how to run the state” ideologies

- **Technocracy** — expert-led decision-making.
    
- **Meritocracy** — rule/advancement by “merit” (often contested on fairness).
    
- **Federalism** — power shared between central and regional governments.
    
- **Confederalism** — looser union of largely sovereign units.
    
- **Deliberative democracy** — legitimacy through public reasoning and deliberation.
    
- **Direct democracy** — citizens vote on policy directly.
